How the Screening Engine Works

NLP vectorization, statistical weighting, and boundary-safe evaluation

1. Multi-Format Text Extraction

Direct parsing of PDF (via PDF.js), DOCX (via Mammoth.js), and TXT files with client-side processing.

2. Text Cleaning & Stopword Filtering

Strips punctuation, numbers, and common English stopwords to isolate high-value domain tokens.

3. TF-IDF Vectorization

Calculates term frequency across documents and penalizes ubiquitous words using Inverse Document Frequency: IDF = ln((N+1)/(DF+1)) + 1.

4. Cosine Similarity & Skill Gap Analysis

Measures angle similarity between JD vector and resume vectors, paired with regex boundary matching for short skills (like C, R, SQL).
